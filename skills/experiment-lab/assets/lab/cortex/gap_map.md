# Gap Map (`gap_map.md`)

> What the cortex should know and does not, one row per question. A gap can be about anything in the field: a method nobody here understands yet, a dataset's limits, a disagreement between sources, a result nobody has tested. `From` names where it came from: reading, an empty area in `index.md`, an experiment, or the user. `Direction` says how it could be answered: by reading (`investigate`), by an experiment, or by asking the user.
>
> Adding knowledge to the cortex needs no gap. When a gap is answered, its row moves down with the claims that answered it. Numbers are not reused.
