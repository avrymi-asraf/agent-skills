# Cortex Index (`index.md`)

> The map of the field, as this cortex knows it. Topic pages are listed under the area of the field they belong to, one line each, updated in the same commit as the page. Read it to find the pages a task needs before opening them, and to see where the cortex is thin. Claims and sources are not listed here: find them with `cortex.py claims --grep <term>` and `cortex.py similar` (`profile.md` §6).

## Steering

* [[purpose]]: what the lab is for, the far goal, how results are judged
* [[gap_map]]: what the cortex should know and does not
* [[log]]: one line per operation on the cortex

## The Field (`topics/`)

> One heading per area of the field named in `purpose.md` §1: its methods, its data, its evaluation practice, its background, the neighbouring fields. Write the areas when the lab starts, before any page exists, and add one whenever knowledge arrives that fits none. An area with no page under it stays listed, with "nothing yet": that is where to read next.
