# Tries of `<experiment name>`

> The map of tries: one line per try, written only in the lab. A try exists when it has a line here, and it is live while its line says `live`; git decides nothing. The try's brief and base are in its plan, on its branch (`notes/<experiment name>/plan.md` → This Try). What lab steps concluded is in `lab-steps.md`.
>
> **Status:** `live` · `merged → <try>` (a reduce took some of its code into that try) · `dropped → <try>` (a reduce took only its lessons) · `dropped` (ended after a lab step) · `closed` (the experiment closed).
> **Comment:** at most 100 characters. While the try is live: what it tries. After it ended: how.
